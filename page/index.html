    Where this comes from, and what the warnings mean

    Each project keeps a status record, written by its own sprint sessions. A refresh, on request and at about 07:40 and 13:20 on weekdays, finds each project's newest record that passes the checker and puts it here. Nothing here changes a project.

    Record from
    When the project wrote what this card shows. A card is only as current as its record.
    Moved on since this record
    The project has changes more than an hour newer than its record, so the card may be out of date.
    No current record
    No record passed the checker. The reason is given; the last valid record is shown if there is one.
    A newer record was refused
    The project wrote a newer record that failed the checker, so an older one is shown.
    Waiting on you
    Everything the projects' records say is waiting on you, oldest first. A line in the margin marks anything waiting more than 7 days.
    Not refreshed
    The last refresh could not reach the project (for Job Scout, your PC), so the card shows the refresh before.
